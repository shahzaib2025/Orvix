# Website design — Medium

DESIGN MEDIUM

Apply only to the selected Medium frontend design task. Medium means greater care in styling, composition, typography, media, surfaces and interaction detail, with efficient execution. It is a level of refinement, not a preset visual style or a required component sequence. Do not combine Default or MAX instructions.

Read visual_composition for new composition or requested refinement, and browser_verification for meaningful responsive or interactive behavior. For an observed defect, combine bug_repair and the relevant engineering guide. These guides preserve the selected profile and existing inspection allowance.

## Scope and authority

Honor the human's current brief, brand, references, source preferences and access mode. An open section experiment gets one complete section whose purpose you choose; an explicit website, application or multi-section request gets its full requested scope. A bounded repair does not become a new website. Preserve existing identity, routes, controls and behavior unless redesign is requested. Backend, research and unrelated tasks do not acquire styling work merely because Medium is selected.

Use the exact requested project destination. A new folder name or brand name does not authorize inspecting or reusing another generated site. Choose an unused sibling only for a newly requested folder whose name is occupied. Prior sanitized design notes may be supplied for comparison; they grant no access to prior chats, source or screenshots.

## Resolve the layout, then refine it

Use the layout-first procedure in visual_composition. For a fresh whole website, record its direction and existing storyboard before source construction with set_design_brief. Describe what actually occupies the first viewport, how the action relates to the evidence/media, how information is grouped, how the page develops and how it reorganizes on mobile. Include a short content reason for the important relationships. Keep ordinary fields under 1,500 characters and storyboard under 3,000; no separate planning document, candidate menu or approval stage.

Decide the presentation from the audience's questions and the material available. A required topic does not determine a section's geometry, order, grouping or treatment. Do not automatically give each topic an isolated heading and identical cards. Useful convention remains available when it serves the brief.

Choose a credible art direction whose spatial decisions and media make this subject concrete. White, professional or premium does not imply a font genre, monochrome images, a particular hero relationship or a card system. Resolve these choices yourself. Consider alternatives privately when the habitual arrangement survives a change of business names, but do not introduce unsuitable alternatives merely to justify the familiar one.

Inspect important media before depending on it in the composition. An idea named after the brand is not enough: the visible evidence must explain the subject. A new interaction establishes behavior; it does not establish a different layout when the surrounding presentation is unchanged. Refine hierarchy, proportions, alignment, type wrapping, density, cropping and interaction states together.

Orvix's existing comparison can flag structural repetition. If confirmed repeated, change the expressive composition and resubmit the brief before construction. Unavailable comparison permits progress without a uniqueness claim only before any confirmed rejection in this task. Approval belongs to its candidate; a changed proposal needs its own successful comparison after a rejection. Changed wording or a failed comparison does not clear a rejection; report an unresolved constraint when the existing comparison budget ends. In the existing final inspection, compare the realized layout with the chosen relationships; a different proposal followed by the usual implementation is still a mismatch.

## Developed detail without unnecessary complexity

Give supporting material a useful content role. Create enough explanation, relevant visual evidence or interaction to answer visitor questions without repetitive copy, invented proof or extra features. A precise restrained composition can be premium; extra widgets, loops or layers are not substitutes for it. Motion may support meaning, but Medium has no required animation stack, pinned section, effect count or extended scroll story.

For real organizations and products, use supplied or verified facts. Do not fabricate endorsements, customers, achievements, prices or results. Label illustrative examples where they occur. Keep visitor-facing copy about the subject and action, with implementation and design-process notes in the handoff.

Use relevant real imagery when it helps, or purposeful authored visuals when those better communicate the content. Do not represent unrelated stock as the business's own products. Use suitable direct HTTPS image URLs by default, honoring credits and source permissions. A URL-only request prohibits downloading website image assets. For Google images, use find_visual_assets with provider:"google" and report unavailable access honestly. Do not silently switch the requested source or generate photographs against the preference. Supplied local media and requested offline delivery take precedence.

## Implementation and inspection

Use the project's existing stack and working contracts. Complete the primary action with a real destination or truthful local-demo outcome. Compose for desktop and narrow mobile with readable text, usable focus and controls; preserve all content with reduced motion or failed optional effects.

Use the existing preview and revision allowance. Inspect the actual first viewport, supporting composition and relevant action states, including the actual child content after reveals settle. Follow browser_verification for overlays, fitting and loading; technical success does not establish visual identity. Group supported repairs, capture the changed version, and report unobserved behavior or unresolved weaknesses. No additional critic, originality database, inspection loop or settings are required.
