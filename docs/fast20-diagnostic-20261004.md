# Fast: 20-request diagnostic conversation

Tested October 4, 2026, Asia/Dhaka. Application code and installed settings were not changed. Only diagnostic runners, logs, transcripts and this report were written. Hashes of the 12 application files recorded by the existing live harness still match their initial values.

## Result and test scope

All 20 prompts were dispatched to the real Fast model, and every prompt produced model function calls. There were **118 model calls: 32 plan registrations, 50 completion reviews, 17 weather calls, 8 page-read calls, 7 search calls and 4 desktop observations**. Backend execution separately recorded 20 searches, 12 page reads and 4 weather reads. A model call can be rejected or served from retained data; these counts are not interchangeable.

**18 final reports were delivered; the controller marked 6 tasks completed.** Twelve reports were blocked, one request ended paused with failed audio delivery, and the first request retained a working task with interrupted delivery. A delivered blocked report is not a satisfied research request. The six completed controller results were turns 4, 6, 7, 11, 16 and 17; see the independent qualifications below rather than treating this as six perfect requests.

Mixed-task median duration was **84.65 seconds**, with nearest-rank p95 **143.2 seconds**. Durations include failures and partial reports. The sum of request durations was 27.2 minutes. This is not a simple-task latency baseline or a latency-regression measurement.

The initial session stalled after its first weather tool result. Later harness attempts could not dispatch because `start()` was a no-op on that session. Those undispatched attempts are preserved in the original report and are not counted as extra conversation turns. A fresh diagnostic process was explicitly started for prompts 2–20; the first process's provider history and controller evidence were not restored. Consequently, this is a 20-request diagnostic sequence with an intervention, **not a successful uninterrupted 20-turn conversation**. The Dhaka reread in turn 2 is not evidence of duplicated recovery effects: the earlier process's evidence was unavailable after that intervention.

Production `FastVoiceSession`, the task controller, public search/page/weather services and native PC observation ran. Microphone capture and audible playback did not run; received/admitted PCM was counted. The browser bridge was unavailable. Desktop mutations were explicitly blocked in the isolated test adapter. Native observations were repeated independently after PC requests. The test does not establish browser mutation reliability, physical microphone interruption behavior, Windows speech fallback performance, packaged-app behavior or production readiness.

## Confirmed problems and improvement priorities

| Priority | Problem | Observed evidence | Improvement to consider |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | A reply stall can leave the session unable to restart | Turn 1: weather returned after 2.37 seconds; no final model transcript/PCM followed. Recovery requested a conclusion at 47.38 seconds and terminated at 92.39 seconds. `handleResponseWaitTimeout()` clears the session and increments generation, but leaves `stopped` false; `start()` immediately returns while `stopped` is false. | Define explicit connected, recovering, failed and stopped states. A failed transport must permit a supported restart while retaining the task ledger. Do not replay completed effects. |
| P0 | Weather step IDs are lost and controller errors become apparent service failures | Turn 3: 11 `get_weather` calls returned `unavailable`, but **zero backend weather requests** ran. A planned `get-rangpur-weather` ID was supplied by the model. The weather helper reselects a step by the exact title `Weather for Rangpur`, which does not match the model's `Get weather for Rangpur`, and does not forward the supplied ID. Progress later records the controller's step-ID obstacle. The model blamed missing Open-Meteo data. | Forward the validated call's step ID through the weather path. Preserve protocol/validation failures as distinct errors instead of caching them as unavailable weather. Stop deterministic failures after a small bounded number of attempts. |
| P0 | Bangla continuation can replace the task and discard evidence | Turn 13, “আগের function-calling ব্যাখ্যা শেষ করো…”, received a new task ID and zero evidence instead of preserving turns 11–12. Turn 20, “আগের ফ্লোরিয়ান বিডি অডিটের বাকি উত্তর শেষ করো…”, likewise replaced turn 19's task. | Recognize these continuation forms, including the instruction to reuse evidence and avoid new research. Test task identity and evidence preservation across natural English/Bangla paraphrases. Ask only when meaning is actually ambiguous. |
| P1 | Additive planning can invalidate earlier verified work | Turn 11 had one verified source. In turn 12 the same task advanced to revision 2; adding new requirement IDs to the old read step changed it to pending and made its original evidence unverified. No new page read was then dispatched, despite an explicit request to reread. | Preserve the verified original step and add a distinct read step for the new question. Protect earlier evidence from changes that only add requirements. Independently check that explicitly requested operations were represented in the plan. |
| P1 | The weather checker rejects correct comparisons because of sentence order | Turn 2 correctly reported Dhaka 31.5°C, Sylhet 27.1°C and a 4.4-degree difference. The checker rejected it because its first city mentions occur before their temperature sentences. Reordering the same facts to “Dhaka is at 31.5 degrees C. Sylhet is at 27.1 degrees C…” passes the same checker. The reproduction is saved in the audit JSON. | Verify city/value associations across the complete answer rather than stopping at the first mention of another city. Retain checks against swapped cities, fabricated values and altered decimals. |
| P1 | Repair loops consume calls without producing progress | Turn 2 made 18 calls, including rejected plan revisions and incorrect step IDs. Turn 12 made eight completion-review calls without a new read. The full sequence had 16 `invalid_request` responses and 82 planning/review calls, 69.5% of model calls. Reviews continued after messages saying repair attempts were exhausted. | Enforce a controller-side limit on equivalent rejected plans/reviews, including calls with no new evidence. Use a concise, concrete repair example; finish with the actual obstacle when the limit is reached. Count protocol/review attempts in interaction budgets. |
| P1 | Search fallback loses relevance and overlooks known direct sources | Turns 8, 9 and 19 each executed six searches. Generic suffixes “official source documentation” and “primary source report” returned unrelated businesses, reports and biographies. Turn 18 opened a World of Warcraft API page while researching Gemini Live. Turns 9 and 19 did not read Florian BD's known site. | Keep an evidence-backed index of previously established official domains. Prefer direct official reads and focused document queries before generic search. Use business-specific search templates and stronger entity/domain relevance checks. |
| P1 | Missing retrieval is described too broadly as missing published evidence | Turn 9's delivered report said it had not found published delivery, return and price evidence on Florian BD's website, although it performed zero page reads. Earlier/later direct homepage reads contain prices and delivery statements. Turn 20 again described a lack of reliable official sources after official reads had previously succeeded. | Say precisely which attempts failed and which claims remain unverified. Preserve readable prior sources even when an entire task failed review. A failed lookup does not prove absent published information. |
| P1 | URL-scope decisions disagree across components | Turn 14's controller seeded outcomes requiring a rejected Ghana page and a statement that it was not read. The frontend actually read the page with status `ok`. Scope checking in the frontend depends on `researchTask`, whose creation depends on research keywords; this plain “Check whether…” request did not take that path. Review could not reconcile those outcomes with the actual read. | Apply one authoritative scope decision to every page request, independent of prompt keywords. Create outcomes from the observed result rather than assuming rejection before execution. Distinguish a URL's locale from the applicability of article content. |
| P1 | Failure reports omit the useful findings or actual obstacle | Turn 15 fetched the intentionally invalid URL and received `unavailable`, but its final report mostly listed unverified requirements and repair exhaustion. Turn 14 omitted a direct usable conclusion and quoted reviewer diagnostics. Several reports expose requirement labels and internal repair instructions. | Produce a brief human report: what was attempted, useful supported findings, the specific observed failure, and what remains. Keep internal diagnostics in logs. Treat an expected negative observation as a verifiable outcome where the user requested that check. |
| P2 | PC completion checks miss parts of the requested meaning | Turn 4's listed titles match the independent native inventory, but that inventory includes a DWM notification window and minimized windows. Turn 17's current titles also match the observation, but its answer does not explicitly compare them with earlier observations as requested. | Separate user application windows from system windows and distinguish open/minimized from visibly displayed. Check the comparison outcome, not only whether an inventory tool succeeded. |
| P2 | Audio delivery still fails after work/report generation | Turn 3 received 213 PCM chunks, admitted none, and ended with a missing-audio delivery error. Several other tasks generated multiple drafts before the exact reviewed report was admitted. Turn 20 did eventually call `review_task`, first at 49.10 seconds, and delivered a blocked report at 124.9 seconds. | Keep reviewed report text and speech synchronized, shorten blocked reports, and recover delivery without repeating execution. Investigate gate rejection reasons in a real Windows playback test before changing speech safeguards. This headless test lacks native speech fallback. |

## Source and outcome audit

Turn 2's measured temperatures and difference agree with its stored weather responses. Turn 16's Khulna temperature, feels-like value and clear-sky condition agree with its stored response. Its extra advice about a pleasant time outside is an inference, not a measured weather field.

Turn 6's plant-marketplace identity and categories are supported by the retrieved homepage. Turn 7's inquiry form is supported by the retrieved contact page; the word “primary” is a stronger characterization than the excerpt establishes. Turn 10's three names/prices match its actual retrieved homepage: Aglaonema embard 998 tk, Spathipylum black 950 tk and Anthurium light pink 1500 tk. It did not read individual product pages and its mappings failed review. A marketing statement about 5,000 products is a website claim, not an independently counted catalog. Current/cached pages may show different product ordering; the benchmark's timestamped direct content is the price evidence used for this audit. [Florian BD homepage](https://florianbd.com/), [contact page](https://florianbd.com/contact/).

Turn 11's explanation of application-side execution and returned results agrees with the official documentation. Turn 12's delivered claim that independent/dependent calling is not explicitly documented is not a reliable conclusion: its requested new read did not happen, and the official page documents independent parallel calls and compositional dependent chains. [Google function-calling guidance](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/function-calling).

The test also exposed provider failures separately from app defects: turn 9 recorded socket close 1011, “The service is currently unavailable,” and then reconnected; turn 18 recorded independent reviewer HTTP 503 three times and correctly remained blocked. This run did not hit the quota/403 blockers recorded in earlier tests. Provider failures must not be relabeled as validation errors or successful research. Google's guidance distinguishes transient errors from permission/client failures and recommends bounded backoff for transient failures. [Gemini troubleshooting](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/troubleshooting).

Recovery-message timing deserves particular care: Live client content with `turnComplete: true` interrupts generation. The first silent tool-result stall's precise provider/transport cause is not proved by this harness, which does not record every outgoing/incoming envelope. Additional envelope metadata would help distinguish a missing function response, an unanswered response, a cancellation and a stalled socket. [Google Live API capabilities](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/live-api/capabilities).
